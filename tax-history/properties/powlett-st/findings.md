## ⚠️ Flag — same pattern as Ocean Grove: company vendor, personal proceeds (not resolved)

Both units were sold with **Thomas Empire Pty Ltd as the legal vendor** (per both statements of adjustment), but the net sale proceeds were paid out directly to **Lloyd personally** (Unit 1) and **"Lloyd & Milani Thomas"** (Unit 2) — not retained by or credited to the company. This is the same shape of discrepancy flagged in the Ocean Grove findings (company/entity holds title, individual receives the economic benefit), just the reverse direction from Inalaa Pty Ltd (there, the entity bought; here, the entity sold and the money went to you personally).

I haven't guessed at what this means. Possibilities include: the company was acting as a bare/nominee owner for you personally, there's an unrecorded loan or distribution from the company to you, or the sale proceeds were meant to be brought to account in the company's books some other way. This is worth putting directly to your accountant — it affects whether any gain/loss on these sales sits with Thomas Empire Pty Ltd (company tax) or with you personally (individual CGT), which in turn affects the "deregister Thomas Empire and keep the losses" plan (see that entity's profile — this may be another asset/liability that needs sorting out before deregistration, on top of the Oxygen Fit Group Trust unit holding already flagged there).
